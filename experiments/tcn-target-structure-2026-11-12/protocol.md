## Fixed metrics, no tuning

For each case and team, report:

1. Adjacent-observed same-slot Euclidean displacement in physical metres. Count, mean, min/max, fixed0.01m histogram quantile intervals at50/90/95/99percentiles (>=200m open upper tail). Fixed descriptive bins `[0,.25,.5,1,2,5,10,20,infinity)` metres; bins carry no plausibility/identity threshold.
2. Rederived encoder velocity clipping proxy: fraction of adjacent-observed pairs with any component `abs(normalizedXYdiff*25)>5`, and fraction of components exceeding5. This is a representation-level clipping proxy, not exact raw-source velocity saturation.
3. Observation appearance/disappearance transitions, visible/missing run counts/mean/max/50/90/95/99percentiles in seconds. Runs reset at real half boundaries; edge fragments retained and explicitly censored. Initial appearance and half-end disappearance are not counted as observed transitions.
4. Visible track-slot count mean and bounded integer count-frequency per frame, plus zero/fewer-than11/more-than11 frame counts. Visible slots are not verified unique players. Allocated team-slot count is a count, not slot IDs;11 is a descriptive football coverage reference, not a release cutoff.
5. Directed nearest-position distance from each currently visible player observation to the previous frame's same-team visible set. This is many-to-one nearest geometry, never assignment/reidentification. Also report it on exactly the adjacent-observed same-slot pairs from item1, so the mean/quantile comparison has matching support. Independent nearest matching can understate motion in crowded regions and cannot identify a switch rate.
6. Same-frame same-team unordered close-position pair counts/rates at exact0m and fixed<=.1/.5/1m, plus fraction of frames containing any such pair. These are close-position proxies; quantization and real proximity can produce them. Never call them duplicate-track truth or repair tracks from them.

No seed search or thresholds fitted from the target. All six cases and both teams are reported; no model choice, correction, production promotion or competition quality gate can depend on these label-free proxies. A large same-slot/nearest-set gap or clipping rate can motivate input-contract/transfer review but is not proof of identity failure. A small gap does not certify serving identity, coverage or target accuracy. Sealed evaluation remains once at the Nov16 freeze under its existing prewritten interpretation, not part of this command.
