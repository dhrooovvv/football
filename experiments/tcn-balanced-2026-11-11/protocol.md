## Decision and the one intervention

Replace the retained parent's IDSSE-only training distribution with ONE fixed mixed-provider distribution: P(IDSSE)=0.5, P(PFF)=0.5, and uniform valid known-window starts conditional on provider. A start w from provider s has probability 0.5/N_s, where N_s is its total valid starts over the fold's training matches. Match probabilities within a provider remain proportional to available starts. No match balancing, class weights, source-dependent loss, schedule, ratio grid or extra training updates. Equal mass is a transparent fixed design choice, not a literature-established or fitted optimum.

The mechanism reference is the completed rejected expansion (same 52-match pool, uniform starts over both providers); relative to that reference, only provider allocation changes. The selection reference remains the **retained best gap-trained 50/50 CatBoost/TCN blend with penalty16**, not the weaker rejected expansion. Comparing against retained best estimates the entire fixed mixed-source training-policy intervention; it does not isolate balancing from introducing PFF. A gain over rejected expansion alone cannot promote anything.

Hypothesis: less domination by PFF may retain more IDSSE control semantics while benefiting from additional trajectories. This may fail: half the retained parent's IDSSE exposure is still replaced; label/noise shift or limited context may dominate. No numeric gain, .8 score or competitive ranking is forecast.
