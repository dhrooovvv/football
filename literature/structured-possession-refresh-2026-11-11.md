## 1. PathCRF: useful new cross-provider evidence, not a drop-in model

[June2026 v2 primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12080v2) adds evidence missing from the earlier v1-centric local summary. Its main Sportec split remains5/1/1matches, test1,831events; AppendixB also evaluates15KLeagueGPSmatches (10/2/3) and seven-match Sportec LOMO. Reported eventF1 is73.88±1.90% withinSportec, but Sportec→KLeague49.16±3.37%; KLeague→Sportec66.25±7.65%. This directly demonstrates annotation/modality transfer risk within the authors' experiment, not our target performance. The method learns player-pair possession paths, with legal edge transitions and sequence likelihood. It trains on ground-truth edge sequences plus auxiliary targets, not only team labels. Known in-play episodes are provided; it does not independently solve full-match dead/live. Prior checkpoint-overlap/licence audit remains unresolved for deployment; no public weights adopted.

**Implication (inference):** structured player-owner reasoning is meaningfully different from global switching penalties, but requires clean player/event supervision or a new latent-state training formulation. Replacing real unknown live/dead with oracle episodes would invalidate our comparison. Persistent player identities in path edges also conflict with unstable target slots unless correspondence is solved. Scratch training and a stopped-track amendment would be necessary. Confidence: moderate mechanism relevance; high confidence in transfer caveat; low confidence of any project gain.

## 2. BallRadar: reconstruct missing ball jointly with possession, with important supervision limits

[Primary2023 full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2306.08206v1) uses15KLeagueGPSmatches plus3Metrica samples. Training includes10GPS+2Metrica matches; validation2GPS+firsthalf of Metrica3; test3GPS+secondhalf of Metrica3, so Metrica validation/test are not match-disjoint. Ten-second windows at10Hz use only known in-play episodes. The hierarchy predicts player possession before ball regression. GPS ball targets are reconstructed from manually annotated events and inferred goalkeeper positions, rather than independently observed dense ball truth. In its imputation experiment, raw missing fraction is randomly varied: before postprocessing,95%masking gives3.0018m position error and91.54%team-possession accuracy, versus5.3536m/83.96% when allballinputs missing. These are in-play frame metrics under that regime, not official spellF1 or target burst-gap evidence. Public code is linked, but no new model/code-licence or checkpoint-provenance admission was performed.

**Implication (inference):** missing-ball recovery could be a separate supervised learning problem using only training-fold observed ball positions as targets; it can add an estimated signal, unlike widening a classifier over unchanged missing inputs. However, reconstructing a single plausible ball path does not establish the correct possession boundary. The paper does not validate calibrated imputation uncertainty. Preserve original observation masks, distinguish estimated from observed coordinates, and never treat imputed coordinates as certain observations. A probabilistic/multiple-hypothesis design would be our proposal, not an established result from this source. It would need stronger temporal/identity contracts, fold-local fitting, realistic burst masks and a reopened representation/data policy. Confidence: moderate conceptual value, low near-term feasibility and gain certainty.

## 3. ASRF: boundary supervision is distinct from simply increasing smoothness

[Primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2007.06866v1) separates frame classification and boundary regression, then refines labels using predicted boundaries. Evaluations cover50Salads(50videos), GTEA(28videos) andBreakfast(over77hours,52individuals), with5-fold/4-fold protocols. On50Salads, its reported F1@50 changes66.7→77.3 with boundary-based refinement (Table3), while ordinary smoothing reaches71.6/70.9 for two tested windows. This supports learned boundaries beyond naive smoothing in those video tasks. The input is pretrained I3D video features; supervision, action durations and metrics differ from football. More stages do not monotonically help: fourstages decline versus three in an ablation, attributed to overfitting. No pretrained model/code reuse or licence admission is made here.

**Implication (inference):** an auxiliary boundary objective could use transitions derived from already available external H/A/D training labels, requiring no new hand labels. It may target inaccurate spell timing, but our dominant missed/short-spell errors are not identical to the paper's oversegmentation problem. A full ASRF decoder/refinement transplant reopens stopped decoder/architecture tracks and adds thresholds. A smaller boundary-trained emissions proposal, if authorized, should preserve the existing decoder and isolate one loss intervention with fixed coefficient/budget. Confidence: moderate task-mechanism rationale; low project benefit certainty.

## Quality-weighted synthesis and recommendation

None of these studies establishes a ceiling, a route to .8, or a comparable full-match H/A/D spellF1. Their different metrics cannot rank their expected project gains. PathCRF v2 is particularly important contrary evidence: a strong within-provider structured model can lose substantially on another provider. BallRadar demonstrates that sparse ball observations help within its protocol, not that hallucinating a ball trajectory reliably solves long hidden events. ASRF shows that explicit boundaries can outperform smoothing on video activities, while exposing overfitting and transfer concerns.

The most feasible new question after current symmetry results is **whether external-label-derived boundary supervision improves the retained emissions without more smoothing**. It uses existing supervision and can be isolated more cleanly than full player-path/imputation pipelines. This is research advice only: three representation failures and decoder/data stops remain binding, so a new training/loss trial requires an explicit bounded amendment and preregistration before user execution. Parent's complementary review of recent boundary/segment regularization should determine whether a stronger modern loss formulation supersedes this old ASRF-inspired mechanism. Do not start it automatically, bundle imputation/attention/extra training, or tune on public/sealed labels.

Longer-term high-upside direction: obtain new reliable evidence through video or independently evaluated ball recovery, then learn possession paths with uncertainty. This could change available information rather than only its classifier, but needs licensed aligned training sources and substantial engineering. Current five development matches are repeatedly reused; independent matches and frozen sealed evaluation are essential to know whether development gains generalize.
