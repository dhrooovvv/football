## What the project evidence establishes

The current retained development recipe reaches mean official spellF1 .602154622 clean / .477681884 target-like corrupted, versus CatBoost16 .517653951 / .323476167. These are different settings; .4777 is the current robust-selection leader, while the historical clean leader is an unaugmented TCN .609882292. There has been measured improvement, followed by diminishing returns and several regressions. It would be inaccurate to call the observed plateau a model-family or information-theoretic ceiling.

The current original TCN has9,635 class-network parameters and17-frame encoded receptive field. A failed deeper/longer model and a failed frame-set encoder do not isolate capacity, optimization, motion loss or domain shift because those interventions changed multiple interacting properties. Supervised PFF expansion and balancing failed. This demonstrates those fixed recipes did not transfer; it does not demonstrate that PFF geometry cannot teach useful representations. All comparisons reuse five development matches, so additional apparent gains can become less reliable as selection continues.

## New directly relevant primary evidence: self-supervised trajectories, then fine-tuning

Rudolph and Brefeld's [Masked autoencoder for multiagent trajectories, Machine Learning2025](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10994-024-06647-3) pretrains by reconstructing masked trajectory segments, then uses the encoder for classification. Their soccer study uses54 proprietary DFL matches at25Hz, five-second complete23-object windows and ten event labels. Pretraining improves event mAP across four labelled-data regimes including all labels. Their split is over halftimes, not our match-level protocol. The architecture uses factorized time/agent attention and assumes complete stable trajectories. It does not report continuous H/A/D spellF1 or our10-second burst-gap setting. Paper licence is CC BY4.0; that does not license proprietary data or implementation. The [authors' project page](https://ml3.leuphana.de/projects.html) links an example PyTorch implementation; we did not fetch it and have not verified an implementation licence or compatible pretrained checkpoint. No turnkey weights were admitted.

**Confidence: high in this paper's finding, low-to-moderate in transfer to our metric.** This is a real soccer pretraining result, unlike generic language-model fine-tuning advice. The useful distinction is learning geometry without importing incompatible possession-label conventions. It can still suffer provider/camera/identity shift. Reconstruction quality is not a substitute for our official downstream gate.

## Bounded project hypothesis, explicitly prospective

If the pending boundary trial fails to yield the required improvement and there is enough time for a further preregistered run, the strongest distinct training direction is **masked geometric pretraining on eligible external training matches, followed by IDSSE-only H/A/D fine-tuning**. It should be ONE fixed pretext task and ONE retained downstream architecture, not a new transformer/decoder/context sweep. A lightweight auxiliary geometric head on the retained encoder would be a project adaptation, not a reproduction of T-MAE, and its limited receptive field may limit transfer. A full factorized transformer is a substantially larger architecture and runtime commitment and should not be disguised as this one change.

For each development fold, allowed pretraining pool would be the48 eligible PFF training matches plus the otherfour IDSSE development matches. Exclude the16 PFF transfer holdouts, both sealed IDSSE matches, the held-out development match even without labels, and all target data. Only valid observed geometry supplies reconstruction targets; interpolate/estimated/missing ball positions must never become observed truth. Fine-tune only on the four training IDSSE H/A/D labels with fold-local preprocessing. Keep current rotation and decoder policies for scoring. This requires an explicit bounded amendment because data-expansion/representation tracks are stopped, plus exact prospective masks, coefficient, steps, seeds, normalization and hashes before execution.

**Extra compute must be measured honestly.** A pretraining stage plus20k supervised updates is not the original20k budget. To attribute gain to pretraining, use a prospectively fixed matched-total-compute from-scratch control. Updates on differently sized pretext and supervised heads need not have equal cost; report actual training time and update/frame counts. Merely beating the20k parent after more total work would show a useful recipe but would not isolate the mechanism. A control followed by a pretraining trial should be sequentially registered, not selected among many unreported arms. No budget or command is locked by this research note.

## Structured segmentation: attractive headline, weak next choice here

The primary [ASOT paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.01518v2) demonstrates postprocessing supervised MS-TCN++ on50Salads: F1@50 rises69.4 to74.9 with accuracy83.5 to83.6. Its transport prior enforces temporal consistency over neighborhoods and includes soft class-balance regularization. It can handle repeated actions without a known action order. This is cooking-video segmental F1, not football spellF1; a half-length sequence also changes computational and scale assumptions. The [official implementation](https://github.com/mingu6/action_seg_ot) declares MIT licensing. No code was downloaded. The paper discusses GPU solving; deterministic MacCPU runtime remains unmeasured.

**Confidence: high in published evidence, low as our best next intervention.** Our weak short-spell recall makes another persistence-heavy postprocessor risky. The decoder track is stopped, and ASOT has several parameters that invite additional reuse of the same five matches. It is a research lead rather than a justified immediate run. Do not convert its unrelated74.9 result into a football performance target.

## Recommendation and stopping discipline

Finish the already registered boundary-objective trial first; it addresses the frame-loss versus spell-selection mismatch with the lowest additional engineering burden. Do not simultaneously launch a seed ensemble, longer training, new loss, reconstruction and transformer. If one further substantive learning route is admitted before freeze, choose the pretrain-then-fine-tune hypothesis with a compute control over another unprincipled architecture increment. Its external evidence supports investigating it, not expecting a large gain or .8.

Set a finite operational budget before preparation: pending boundary trial plus at most one subsequent learning route and its necessary compute control, contingent on completion before release qualification. This is a recommendation for the parent, not an amendment to the user's gate/stop rules. If it cannot fit, use the current winner and release it correctly. The largest unresolved potential new information remains synchronized visible-ball evidence, but no admitted external video+continuous-possession evaluation path has been established. More research alone cannot establish a new ceiling or a winning score.

High-impact unknowns: boundary trial outcome; whether PFF geometry is normalized and observed consistently enough for the pretext task; whether the original encoder's short field can learn transferable masked geometry; target slot churn damage; Mac runtime; whether an apparent five-match gain survives the once-only sealed evaluation. Those unknowns can invert the recommendation.
