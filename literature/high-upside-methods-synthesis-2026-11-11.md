## Most relevant new paper: improve the training objective

[Mitsuoka and Hotta, CVPR2026 Workshop, primary full text](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.01859v1) studies boundary supervision plus segment consistency on GTEA,50Salads andBreakfast across three backbones. Its modest output addition and training losses improve segmental measures without comparable changes in frame accuracy. Hyperparameters are dataset-specific. This is evidence for objective design on other tasks, not football or official spellF1. No runnable licensed author implementation/checkpoint was verified or adopted. CVF requests returned403; primary arXiv full text was accessible. It is an April2026 work newly identified for this project, not a last30days release.

**Project inference and prospective single change:** current training optimizes frame crossentropy while selection measures complete H/A spells. The saved short-spell recall and missed-spell counts justify examining explicit training supervision at transitions. Preserve the useful original trajectory encoder rather than discard its motion/history again. Keep decoder16 and the newly retained rotation policy fixed. Do not add the paper's second segment-consistency loss, new smoothing/refinement, more data, larger context or extra updates simultaneously.

This is not yet an exact protocol: boundary definition/width, fixed loss coefficient, class-imbalance handling, X/half/crop-edge masking and optimizer/RNG/initialization accounting must be prospectively locked before running. A boundary target must never be derived from held-out or target labels for inference. A head discarded at inference is still a changed training objective and needs proper provenance. Existing track stops require an explicit bounded plan amendment; a loss is not a loophole to resume an architecture sweep.

## Practical incremental alternative: seed diversity

The decision reviewer ranks one fixed two-seed ensemble first for evaluation simplicity. It preserves the successful architecture and may reduce random initialization variance; errors may also be strongly correlated. Each member20k is not the same total training budget as one20kmodel. Exact additional seed/fold provenance, equal weights, total cost and unchanged view policy must be declared; no best-seed selection or sweep. This has credible generic evidence, not proven large possession gains. It is a different candidate from boundary supervision; do not run both together.
